A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before your
first take.

Is it free and open source?

Yes. The source is available under the MIT license. There are no artificial project or recording length limits. Available disk space and your Mac determine capacity.

Which Macs can run it?

The current download targets Apple silicon Macs running macOS 15 or later. Node.js is included with the app. This is an early preview; a notarized public release is still pending.

Does my recording leave my Mac?

Recording, editing and Whisper transcription run locally. Model downloads need an internet connection. Optional cloud assistance sends the necessary transcript, edit context and your prompt to the provider you choose.

Can I edit after exporting?

Absolutely. Export produces a separate video. The project retains its original media, camera intervals, effects and captions for future edits.

What permissions does it need?

Screen recording, camera and microphone permissions depend on what you capture. Input Monitoring adds optional typing activity. macOS manages these permissions in Privacy & Security.

How do I get help or contribute?

Open an issue on GitHub, suggest an improvement, or email info@serkanuslu.com. Contributions and thoughtful feedback are welcome.
